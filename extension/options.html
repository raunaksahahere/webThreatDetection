WTD Companion settings

The extension talks only to your local WTD server at http://127.0.0.1:8000. By default it sends a URL only when you click "Check this page" or "Check this link". It never reads page content, cookies or form data.

Warn before opening risky pages (optional)

When on, the address of every page you open in a tab is sent to your local WTD server for an address-only check (URL rules and the local CERT Polska list; the page is not fetched and nothing goes to third parties). Local and private addresses are never sent. High Risk or Confirmed Malicious pages are replaced by a warning. The browser will ask for the "read your browsing history" permission, which is needed to see page addresses; you can turn this off at any time.
